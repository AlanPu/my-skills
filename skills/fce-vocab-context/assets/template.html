① 英文原文 点击高亮词查看讲解 · 蓝色 = FCE 核心词 · 黄色 = 高级搭配

Tu Youyou was born in Ningbo in 1930. From an early age, she showed remarkable talent for science. After graduating from university, she dedicated herself to the study of traditional Chinese medicine. In 1969, she was appointed to lead a secret research project, and she worked tirelessly for years, testing hundreds of ancient remedies. Her extraordinary persistence finally paid off: she discovered artemisinin, a drug that has saved millions of lives. In recognition of her outstanding contribution, she was awarded the Nobel Prize in 2015. Today she remains a source of inspiration to young scientists all over the world.

② 中文译文 画线处与英文高亮一一对应

屠呦呦 1930 年出生于宁波。从小，她就展现出非凡的科学天赋。大学毕业后，她投身于中医药研究。1969 年，她被任命领导一个秘密研究项目，多年来她不知疲倦地工作，测试了数百种古代药方。她非凡的坚持终于得到了回报：她发现了青蒿素——一种已经拯救了数百万人生命的药物。为表彰她杰出的贡献，2015 年她被授予诺贝尔奖。如今，她仍然是全世界年轻科学家们的灵感源泉。

③ 你的句子 → 升级版 对比你自己的作文，看差距在哪一个词上

你写的He was a great Chinese scientist. / He spent many years researching the nuclear submarine.
升级He was a remarkable Chinese scientist who dedicated many years to researching the nuclear submarine.
① great → remarkable（正式、更高级）② spend time doing → dedicate time to doing（to 是介词，后接动名词）③ 用定语从句把两个短句合并，句子立刻有层次。
你写的…he still work very hard.
升级…he worked tirelessly for decades.
语法硬伤：he + 过去时，必须是 worked；同时 work very hard 太平淡，换成 work tirelessly 立刻上一个档次。
你写的Because of his huge devotion to the research, people call him "The father of …".
升级Thanks to his extraordinary dedication, he is widely regarded as "The Father of …".
because of → thanks to（含"多亏了"的庆幸语气）；people call him 太口语，改被动 be widely regarded as 更客观正式；huge → extraordinary。
你写的He also received the Medal of the Republic in 2019.
升级In recognition of his achievements, he was awarded the Medal of the Republic in 2019.
receive → be awarded（被动语态更正式）；加上 In recognition of + 名词，把"为什么得奖"一句话交代清楚，句式也高级了。

④ 写作迁移：人物介绍类作文的 8 步骨架 FCE Part 2 Article 约 140–190 词，本文 99 词正好是「人物简介段」的骨架

  1. X was born in + 地点 + in + 年份. / X came from a poor family in … 交代出生地与年份，一句话带过，别铺开写。
  2. From an early age, he/she showed a remarkable talent for … 自幼显露天赋 —— 比 "When he was young, he liked…" 高级得多。
  3. After graduating from …, he/she dedicated himself/herself to … 毕业后投身某项事业。to 后面跟名词或 doing。
  4. In 1969, he/she was appointed to lead / was chosen to head … 关键转折事件，用被动语态显得客观正式。
  5. Despite + 名词/doing, he/she worked tirelessly … 先写困难，再写努力，制造对比。Despite 后不接完整句子。
  6. Thanks to his/her outstanding contribution, … / His/Her persistence finally paid off when … 结果句。两个万能句式任选，都自带"努力→收获"的逻辑。
  7. In recognition of his/her achievements, he/she was awarded … 荣誉奖项，附上"为表彰……"的原因，信息更完整。
  8. Today he/she remains a source of inspiration to … 结尾升华。比 "He is a good example for us" 强太多。

⑥ 六个高频易错点

易错正确说明
✗ dedicate oneself to do sth✓ dedicate oneself to doing sth这里 to 是介词，不是不定式符号。同理 be used to doing / look forward to doing。
✗ persist to do sth✓ persist in doing sthpersist 固定跟 in。名词 persistence 常用 show great persistence。
✗ contribution for sth✓ contribution to sth动词 contribute to 也是 to。make a contribution to 是万能搭配。
✗ thanks for his help, we finished…✓ thanks to his help, we finished…thanks to = 多亏（表示原因）；thanks for = 谢谢（表示感谢）。别混。
✗ He awarded the prize.✓ He was awarded the prize.漏掉 be 意思就反了 —— 变成"他给别人颁奖"。主动是 award sb sth。
✗ in recognition for his work✓ in recognition of his work固定搭配 in recognition of，用于颁奖、致谢、纪念等正式场合。
